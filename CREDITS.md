# Credits

## Gun sounds

Every gunshot and gun-handling sound (`assets/mymod/sounds/*_shot*.ogg`, rack, slide, bolt, magazine, pump, shell,
feed cover, belt, revolver and misfire sounds) is cut from these recordings by `tools/import_gun_sounds.py`, which
lists the exact source file for each sound.

- **Snake's Authentic Gun Sounds** by SnakeF8 (F8 Studios):
  https://f8studios.itch.io/snakes-authentic-gun-sounds
  Free for commercial use, no credit required (the author releases them to the public domain). Supplies the .22LR,
  5.56, 7.62x39 and 7.62x54R shots, the AR, AK, .308 bolt, pump and break-action handling.
- **Snake's SECOND Authentic Gun Sounds** by SnakeF8 (F8 Studios):
  https://f8studios.itch.io/snakes-second-authentic-gun-sounds-pack
  Same terms. Supplies the 9mm, .308 and 20 gauge shots, the pistol, revolver and magazine handling.
- **Gunshot sounds** on OpenGameArt: https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
  Listed as CC0 on OpenGameArt; the archive's own note reads "Copyright (c) 2009 Vincent Sevedge", Creative Commons
  Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), so it is credited here either way. Supplies the
  Mosin Nagant (pipe rifle) and SKS (junk repeater) shots.
- **Gun reload sounds** by SpringySpringo on OpenGameArt, CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
  Reviewed as a source; not used in the current mix.
