# TOMYEN 0.3.0: Corpse Run

Die and go get your stuff back from the Isle of the Damned, fuse items in fae ponds, and race a bathtub. This is still an early alpha, so back up your worlds before updating.

Requires **Fabric Loader 0.19.5+**, **Fabric API** and **Minecraft 26.3** (Java 25).

## The Isle of the Damned
- **Die and your belongings go to the Isle,** a foggy death dimension. A gravestone marks where you fell: sneak and hold right-click on it for 5 seconds (or offer a holy or soul item) to go down after them.
- **Your Revenant,** a ghostly copy of you in your death gear, guards your loot. Beat it for a Soul Cache and a rift home, or leave by the Lantern of Return. Die on the Isle and your loot sinks a depth: Shore, Hollows, Abyss.
- **Shades and the Soul Tide:** notable mobs that died come back as ghostly shades, and routine kills (mob farms) feed a tide that sends bigger waves. Shades drop Ectoplasm and Soul Charges.

## Item fusing
- Throw two items and a Fae Dust into a fae pond to fuse them (70% chance; bad pairs are spat back), or use the Exotic-tier **Fusion Reactor**, which never fails. Fairies and villagers drop riddles about what fuses.
- 21 fusions: Swordfish Sword, Fish Fish Fish, Rocket Boots, Murder Wings, Stormcrow Rig, Barbel Lash, Grave Auger, Hexed Rod, Whiskerline Rod, Writ of Absolution, Slide Hammer, Antler Pike, Tuskaxe, Moonblade, Pufferblade, Gnome Lance, Leviathan Blade, Phantasmal Chestplate, Wolfhide Carrier, Astral Visor and Revenant Mail (once a day, dying in it keeps your gear off the Isle).

## Vehicles
- **Bathtub Boat:** a clawfoot tub with an outboard motor. Twice as fast as a boat on kerosene, rows when the tank is empty, waddles on land, and has a rubber duck horn (H).

## Known issues
- The Isle hasn't been tested in multiplayer, or with Revenants that died holding a gun.
- The Moonblade may still deal its night bonus during the day.
- Some fusions were merged before their in-game tests finished, so expect rough edges. Patches will follow.

# TOMYEN 0.2.0: Creature Feature

Ten new mobs, a river boss and a fish called Fish Fish. This is still an early alpha, so back up your worlds before updating.

Requires **Fabric Loader 0.19.5+**, **Fabric API** and **Minecraft 26.3** (Java 25).

## New mobs
- **Shrimp with a Gun:** a human-sized shrimp that comes out of oceans and onto beaches at night. It fires four-round bursts from a pistol, then has to reload, which is your chance to rush it. It drops Raw Shrimp (food and ocean-fish bait) and, rarely, its Shrimp Pistol.
- **Gnome army:** garden gnomes are statues by day. At night a Gnome Captain leads a squad out of fae forests to steal your ripe crops in formation. Scarecrows keep them away. Kill the captain and the squad flees. Smash a statue at dawn to keep it as a Garden Gnome block.
- **The Accountant:** a slime in a suit who lives by village ATMs. Take out emerald loans from him, and expect audits. If your debt is overdue, he pays it down with the emeralds in your pockets. Kill him and the bank bills you, and he splits into Junior Accountants. He drops the new Extension Form, which buys you an extra day on your debt.
- **Josh:** a villager named Josh who sells only Fish Fish. About one village in eight gets one, or you can make your own with a Fish Fish Crate.
- **Bog Hag:** a swamp witch who throws hexes and calls up slimes. Her Hag's Curse slows mining, spoils ore, and makes fish bite late and fight harder. Milk won't fix it: brew Hexbreaker Tea from water mint in the Kettle. She drops herbs, Bog Muck (bait) and the Hag Charm rod lure.
- **Grave Digger:** a zombie who tunnels under your walls when he can't reach you. Listen for digging under the floor. Cobblestone, planks, bricks, obsidian and metal stop him. He drops the Gravedigger's Shovel, which digs faster at night.
- **Banshee:** a floating ghost whose wail blinds you and sends nearby undead after you. Normal weapons do half damage, and any holy weapon kills her in one hit. She drops Ectoplasm, used for Soul Charges.
- **Wild Boar:** paws the ground, then charges, and is dazed if it hits a wall or your shield. Tusks make the Tusk Spear, Tusk Harpoons for the Harpoon Gun, and a Tusk Hook.
- **Crow Murder:** flocks that strip farms and steal shiny items, sometimes right out of your hand. They stash loot in a treetop Crow Nest you can raid. Scarecrows keep them off. Crow Feathers make the Crow Fly lure and arrows.
- **Moose:** big taiga and swamp animals. Bulls charge and cows stomp. Calm one with food and saddle it to ride through snow and water. Antlers make Antler Handles for tougher tools, and an Antler Hook.
- **Giant Catfish (The River Monster):** a rare river boss. Hook it at night (Stink Bait helps) and it surfaces to fight, overturning boats and dragging you underwater. Harpoons pull it in and stun it. It drops a giant trophy for the new Giant Fish Mount, Catfish Whiskers for Whisker Line, and an Ancient Hook.

## Fishing
- **Fish Fish:** a new and extremely generic fish, common in rivers, oceans and most temperate biomes.

## Fixes
- Night vision from the Neuralink, the Cathode TV Helmet and Star Steel gear no longer flashes.

# TOMYEN 0.1.0: First Public Test

**The Only Mod You'll Ever Need.** Meteors, fae, werewolves, rogue machines and UFOs are invading, and this is everything you get to fight back. This is an early alpha. Expect bugs and balance changes, and keep backups of your worlds.

Requires **Fabric Loader 0.19.5+**, **Fabric API** and **Minecraft 26.3** (Java 25).

## Guns and gear
- **Real firearms:** Glock 17, M1911, .357 Revolver, Snub Revolver, MP5, MAC-10, M4 Carbine, AKM, Galil, AWP, M249 SAW, Remington 870, SPAS-12, Lever-Action Rifle, Sawed-off, Nail Gun, Harpoon Gun, Flare Gun and Slingshot.
- **Gun features:** magazines you load and swap, fire modes, aim down sights, weapon inspect, recoil and spread, real recorded gun sounds, and keyframed reload animations in first and third person.
- **Ammo for every caliber,** including FMJ, hollow point, AP, silver, holy silver, tungsten and depleted-uranium rounds.
- **Attachments:** suppressors, muzzle brakes, compensators, red dots, holo sights, Kobra and hunting scopes, lasers, flashlights, grips, a bipod, a sling, a bayonet, extended and drum magazines, and a Glock switch.
- **Weapon Station:** builds every gun from crafted parts.
- **Homemade early guns:** Pipe Pistol, Pipe Rifle, Blunderbuss and Junk Repeater.
- **Heavy weapons:** RPG-7 with homing rockets, the M2 Flamethrower, napalm, and a power armor Minigun.
- **Energy weapons:** Laser Rifle, Railgun and Ray Gun, salvaged from the aliens.
- **Wacky weapons:** Fish Gun, Leaf Blower / Blower-Vac, Nose Hair Whip, Trombone (play songs that hex enemies and rally allies) and the Soul Vacuum.
- **Armor:** plate carriers with Level I to III plates, Hazmat suit, Scrap armor, Cathode TV Helmet (with night vision) and Big Red Boots.
- **Mobility:** Soda-Bottle Jetpack and Grapple Claw.

## Vehicles and suits
- **Tank:** a full-size drivable main battle tank with a crew HUD, manual loading, thermal sights and smoke launchers.
- **Bulwark Walker:** a mech with swappable arm weapons, built in the Mech Gantry.
- **Power Armor Mk I to III:** fusion cores, a repulsor and a Unibeam.
- **Hogfire:** a flying, rocket-powered pig that takes a gun mount.
- **Exoskeleton:** module sockets for armor, elytra and servo gear.
- **Servo gear:** Servo Arm Backpack and Servo Drone.

## Invaders and new mobs
- **UFO invasion:** UFOs with tractor beams, and Aliens.
- **Meteorites:** falling meteors and crash sites carrying Meteoric Iron, Star Steel and star crystals.
- **Werewolves:** cursed villagers turn on their village every few nights.
- **The Feywild:** Fae Rings lead to a new dimension with fairies, fae silver and fishing ponds.
- **Rogue machines:**
  - the Cathode Walker, a CRT television on human legs;
  - Skullcranes and the Skullcrane Pack Leader;
  - the Shopping Cart.
- **Undead and evil:** Vampires, countered by the Crucifix and silver.
- **The weird stuff:**
  - The Schnozzle, a rare flying nose.
  - The Big Banana, a three-block-tall walking banana.
  - The banker villager, with credit cards, bank vaults and a debt-collection raid if you don't pay.

## Fishing
- **Fishing minigame:** a quick-time reeling fight that gets harder with the fish's weight and rarity.
- **41 fish species** spread across biomes, dimensions, times of day and weather. Every fish has a realistic weight range, and many swim as live, bucketable fish.
- **Rods:** 11 rods, from a stick-and-string pole to Star Steel, customised with lines, hooks, reels, bobbers and lures at the **Tackle Bench**.
- **Baits:** earthworms, crickets, chum and more, plus bait for trophy-sized catches.
- **Fish Mount:** shows off your biggest catches.
- **Catches beyond fish:** junk, treasure, tackle boxes and an old boot.

## Materials and crafting
- **New metals with full tool and armor sets:** Bloom Steel, Silver and Holy Silver, Magmasteel, Soul Steel, Froststeel, Voidsteel, Star Steel and Fae Silver.
- **Long swords:** one for every tier.
- **Dragonscale armor:** made from dragon fossils.
- **Industry:**
  - Steel, aluminium, duralumin, rubber and oil, plus kerosene and rocket fuel.
  - Tungsten and uranium, with a Centrifuge and radiation.
- **Early-game stations:**
  - the Bloomery, a crude first-hour forge;
  - the Scrap Bench, which builds from Blueprints;
  - Herbalism, with eight wild herbs, a kettle, a mortar, teas and herbal wards.
- **Late game:** the Star Forge infuses gear under the night sky.
- **Drills:** powered mining drills with swappable bits, plus drill-only rock.

## Survival and utility
- **Scavenger's kit:** crowbar, bandages, first aid kits, a canteen, a satchel, Molotov cocktails, pipe bombs, traps and an alarm.
- **Detectors and trackers:** Prospector's Pendulum, Metal Detector, Meteorite Scanner, Dowsing Rods, UFO Radar, Geiger Counter, Lunar Almanac and Ledger.
- **Scarecrows:** guard your crops and decoy mobs and UFOs.
- **Neuralink:** brain implants with modules for ESP, night vision, ore X-ray, telekinesis, blink, EMP and more.

## Good to know
- Everything is in the **TOMYEN** and **Industry** creative tabs.
- The keybinds are grouped under **TOMYEN** in Controls: fire mode, inspect, tank controls, power armor, the fishing reel and more.
- The real gun sounds come from CC0 sound packs. Thanks to their creators.
